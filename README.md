# atlas-film

The medium of the Eidograph: the historic process table and its
response curve (cyanotype, vandyke, platinum, salt, albumen, silver,
gum), the tricolour pigment model, and the Beer–Lambert paper law —
the numbers with provenance that say what a process, pigment or paper
does to light and deposit.

Pure numpy. No GL, no plates, no darkroom import: the darkroom is the
instrument that applies these models, not a dependency of them. The
sibling repositories are atlas-optical (the glass) and atlas-darkroom
(the instrument that brings the parts together).

Born 2026-08-28 by extraction from the darkroom's develop monolith,
history carried, every print bit-identical across the move — the gate
is the darkroom's `tools/film_golden.py`, and the plan it followed is
`docs/film-extraction.md` there. `PLAN.md` here is the model half:
what the medium still owes, and the organs that will pay it.
